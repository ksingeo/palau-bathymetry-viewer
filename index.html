About this data

Depths are reconstructed from the colour-shaded map figures (Figures 6–10) in Ferrier-Loh, A. and P. L. Colin. 2025. Bathymetry of Palau's Outer Reef Slope. Technical Report, Coral Reef Research Foundation. Colours were converted back to depth using each figure's legend, so values are approximate: pixel-level noise is about ±1% of each figure's depth scale, and the figures' own rendering adds further error that hasn't been measured. Very shallow water near the reef crest can't be resolved from the colours, so it is reported as "less than" a limit.

Vertical datum: MLLW (Malakal tide station). Horizontal: WGS 84 / UTM 53N, reprojected for display.

Not for navigation.
