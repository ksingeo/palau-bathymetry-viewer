About this data

The map shows the Coral Reef Research Foundation's published outer-reef bathymetry (Outer Reef web experience), surveyed with multibeam sonar from 2019 to 2023 and combined with deep-water Scripps/NOAA data.

CRRF publishes the depths as coloured map tiles, not as numbers, so the depth readout is decoded from those colours on a ~19 m grid. Two independent CRRF renderings agree to within about 5–10%. Treat readings as approximate. Water deeper than a section's colour scale is filled in from the figures in the 2025 technical report where possible.

Vertical datum: MLLW (Malakal tide station). Map copyright © 2026 Coral Reef Research Foundation. No guarantee is made as to accuracy. Not for navigation.
